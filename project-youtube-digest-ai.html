YouTube Digest AI

Personalized YouTube Intelligence & Daily Video Summaries

Watch Less. Know More.

YouTube Digest AI is an AI-powered content summarization platform that automatically monitors your personally selected YouTube channels and delivers concise, personalized daily summaries of newly published videos, helping users stay informed while saving valuable time.

What the platform provides

  • Personalized Channel Monitoring

    Users select their favorite YouTube channels and content creators, creating a customized feed of topics and sources they care about.

  • AI Video Summarization

    Automatically analyzes new videos and generates concise summaries highlighting key topics, insights, and takeaways.

  • Daily Intelligence Digest

    Delivers a consolidated daily overview of new content across all selected channels, eliminating the need to watch every video.

  • Smart Content Selection

    Helps users quickly identify which videos are worth watching in full, with direct links to the original content.

YouTube Digest AI transforms hours of YouTube content into minutes of reading, allowing users to stay informed without information overload.
