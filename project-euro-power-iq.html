Euro Power IQ

AI-Powered European Electricity Market Intelligence & Forecasting

Open Euro Power IQ

Euro Power IQ is an AI-driven electricity market intelligence platform covering European energy markets, with a particular focus on Serbia and Southeast Europe.

What the platform provides

  • Electricity Market Analytics

    Historical and current electricity prices across European markets, with interactive charts and market comparisons.

  • Balancing Market Analytics

    Comprehensive balancing electricity price data, trends, and visualizations.

  • AI Price Forecasting

    Seven-day AI-powered forecasts for electricity and balancing market prices.

  • AI Data Agent & Analytics

    Explore, filter, aggregate, and analyze the entire energy database using natural language, with automatic generation of downloadable CSV datasets and interactive graphs.

Euro Power IQ combines market data, predictive analytics, and conversational AI into one powerful platform, helping energy traders, producers, investors, and market participants make faster, better-informed decisions.
